KONTEXT: 2 Years

Light Design · Haus der Visionäre, Berlin, 21 August 2026

Light design and operation for KONTEXT 2 Years, the second anniversary of KONTEXT at Haus der Visionäre, Berlin: twelve hours across four stages, with 24 artists. The night featured live sets by Ssaliva, Emma DJ and Valeria Litvakov, and the premiere of The Spirit by Buttechno & Triš.
