companions: Culterim Spaces

Light Design · provessel, Culterim Spaces, Berlin, 12 November 2026

Lighting design for the exhibition companions with works by Min-Jia, Brad Nath and Hou Ching, and its opening performances by Josefina Maro and Otay:onii, at Culterim Spaces in a former Rossmann, Berlin. The light was built around the square, office-like structure of the building.
