Kyiv Biennial: KW

Light Design · Berlin, 2025

At KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Berlin, designed and programmed live lighting for the concert week of Kyiv Biennial's "A Bird That Cannot Land," crafting artist-specific lighting for performances by Abdullah Miniawy, Heinali, Andriana-Yaroslava Saienko, Ihor Tsymbrovsky, Katarina Gryvul, Nazanin Noori, Nour Sokhon, and Wafaa Saied.
