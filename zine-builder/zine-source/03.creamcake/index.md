Working with light designer *Shaly López*, developed spatial previsualization and simulation using Capture Lighting and Blender, alongside live lighting operation with GrandMA3 and live laser programming in TouchDesigner throughout 3hd 2025: Vertigo Festival for [*Creamcake*](https://creamcake.de) in Berlin. Worked with performing artists including *TOURS*, *Mug (Ludwig Wandinger & Yves B Golden)*, *Joshua Woolford w/ Sippin’ T & Lie Ning*, *Star & Stan*, and *Kinlaw*, developing site-specific lighting compositions and immersive stage environments tailored to each artist’s performance and sonic identity.
