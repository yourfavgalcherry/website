Funded by the EU in collaboration with *DE/MO Foundation* and *Public Art Lab Berlin*.

An immersive audiovisual installation exploring light pollution and its disorienting effects on nocturnal pollinators. Presented at [TINCON](https://tincon.org/) / [re:publica](https://re-publica.com/en) in Berlin, combining ecological research with spatial storytelling through ILDA laser, DMX lighting control, and projection mapping programmed in TouchDesigner.
