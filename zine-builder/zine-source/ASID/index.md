Lighting programming, design, and live operation using GrandMA2 during the Drowned God event at *Musikbrauerei*, Berlin, organized by *Fetchish*, *EWASTE*, [ASID](https://www.instagram.com/aphoticsignals/), and *Tear City*. Executed real-time lighting control across multiple live performances, integrating scenographic and audiovisual elements within the industrial architecture of the venue.
